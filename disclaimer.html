Disclaimer & disclosures

How Fresh.Energy makes money, how quote requests are shared and the limits of our calculators.

Advertising disclosure

Fresh.Energy displays ads served by Google AdSense. Ads are labelled and are separate from editorial content. Advertisers do not influence our guides or calculator results.

Lead-sharing disclosure

When you submit a quote request and tick the consent box, we may share your details with up to three independent providers who serve your area. These providers may pay Fresh.Energy for introductions. This is how we keep the service free. You can withdraw consent at any time via our contact page.

Affiliate and sponsorship disclosure

Some links may be affiliate links, and some content may be sponsored. Sponsored content and paid placements are always labelled. We only feature products and services we believe are relevant to readers.

Calculator disclaimer

Calculators provide estimates using the assumptions shown on each page. Real-world results depend on your site, equipment, installation quality, weather, usage, utility tariffs and incentive eligibility. Use results as a starting point for conversations with qualified professionals.

Incentives disclaimer

Incentives, tax credits and rebates change frequently and have eligibility rules. Our summaries are for orientation; always confirm with the official program administrator or a tax professional.

Safety

Electrical, gas and roof work can be dangerous and is often regulated. Use licensed professionals and obtain required permits.

Support and donations

Fresh.Energy is not a registered charity. Contributions are voluntary, are not tax-deductible and do not purchase editorial influence.
