Official rules (summary)

  1. No purchase necessary. A purchase does not improve your chances. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.
  2. Eligibility: individuals aged 18+ (or the age of majority in their jurisdiction). Employees and partners of Fresh.Energy and their households are not eligible.
  3. Entry period: Round 1 runs from 00:00 UTC November 15, 2026 to 23:59 UTC January 31, 2027. One entry per person per contest.
  4. Skill-based judging: winners are chosen by a panel using the published criteria — not by chance. Bill Cut entries must provide verifiable bill data on request.
  5. Prizes: as listed per contest, delivered as digital gift cards or equivalent value. Prizes are not transferable; we may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Winners are responsible for any taxes.
  6. Winners are announced by February 15, 2027 and notified by email; they must respond within 14 days or an alternate is selected. Canadian residents may be required to answer a skill-testing question.
  7. Licence: you keep ownership of your entry and grant Fresh.Energy a non-exclusive licence to display it on this site and our channels with credit.
  8. Privacy: entry details are used only to run the contest — see our privacy policy.
