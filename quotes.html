Frequently asked questions

Does it cost anything?

No. Quotes are free and there is no obligation to buy. Installers who join our network pay us, which keeps the service free for homeowners.

How many companies will contact me?

Up to three vetted providers that serve your area and the services you selected — never a list of dozens. Tell us your preferred contact method and time and we pass that on.

What should I have ready?

A recent electricity bill (or your average monthly amount), your roof's age and orientation if you want solar, and a rough timeline. Photos of your electrical panel help installers price batteries and EV chargers accurately.

Can I just get an estimate without being contacted?

Yes — use our calculators. They are free and never ask for contact details.

How do you vet installers?

Partners must hold the licences and insurance their region requires, show a track record of completed installs, and agree to our homeowner code: written itemised quotes, no high-pressure tactics and no misleading savings claims. Complaints are reviewed and partners can be removed.
